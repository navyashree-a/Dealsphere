Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

DealSphere (dealsphere.pages.dev) is run by Tamada Media in India. This page explains what information DealSphere collects when you use it, why, and the choices you have.

  • You can browse DealSphere without signing in. We only collect personal information if you sign in.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • When you open a deal, you go to the store's own website, where the store's privacy policy applies.

Information we collect

When you…We collectWhy
Sign in with Google Your name, email address, profile photo link and Google account ID, as shared by Google. To sign you in and show your name and photo on your profile.
Sign in with WhatsApp Your mobile number. To send you a one-time sign-in code on WhatsApp and to sign you in.
Save deals or set price alerts Which deals you saved, and your alert prices. So they're still there the next time you sign in.
Stay signed in A sign-in cookie (ds_session). To keep you signed in for up to 30 days, or until you sign out.

A profile photo you choose from your device stays in your browser and isn't uploaded to us. The email and password form on the sign-in page isn't active yet: what you type there isn't sent to or stored by us.

How long we keep it

  • WhatsApp sign-in codes are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form and expire after 5 minutes. A record of how many codes were sent to a number is kept for up to 1 hour, to prevent abuse.
  • Your sign-in session lasts up to 30 days and ends when you sign out.
  • Your account details, saved deals and price alerts are kept until you remove them or ask us to delete them.

Services that help run DealSphere

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores the information above. Like any website host, it processes technical details such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google" and the fonts used on the main site, so Google receives your IP address when the site loads.
  • Pinnacle, a WhatsApp Business provider, sends sign-in codes to your number through WhatsApp (Meta).

We share information with these services only so they can provide these functions for DealSphere.

Stores and affiliate links

When you open a deal, you leave DealSphere for the store's website (for example Amazon or Flipkart). Some links may be affiliate links: if you buy something, the store or an affiliate network may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. The store and affiliate network may use their own cookies to record that you came from DealSphere. Their privacy policies apply to what happens on their sites.

Your choices

  • Remove saved deals and price alerts at any time from your profile and notifications.
  • Sign out at any time to end your session.
  • Ask us to see, correct or delete your information by emailing us (below).
  • For Google sign-in, you can also remove DealSphere's access in your Google Account settings.

Children

DealSphere is meant for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

We use secure (HTTPS) connections, and sign-in cookies that page scripts can't read. We store only scrambled (hashed) versions of sign-in codes and session tokens. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
